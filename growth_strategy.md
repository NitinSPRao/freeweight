# Freeweight Growth Strategy
**Columbia University Athletics — Initial Market Entry**

---

## Executive Summary

Freeweight is a strength training platform designed to modernize the relationship between athletes and coaches. By streamlining workout planning, execution tracking, and coach-athlete communication into a single tool, Freeweight eliminates the friction that today's strength coaches face managing multiple sports, dozens of athletes, and outdated workflows.

We're starting at Columbia because we're Columbia athletes. We've lived this problem firsthand — getting workouts over text, logging lifts in notes apps, having no real channel to communicate with our coaches outside of practice. We're building the tool we always wanted, and we're starting with the coaches and teams we already know.

**Goal:** Get 3–5 Columbia strength coaches using Freeweight as their primary planning and communication tool. Use that foundation to build case studies and expand to other Ivy League and D1 programs.

---

## The Problem: Coaching in Fragments

Today's collegiate strength coaches cobble together multiple tools to do their job. This fragmentation creates real costs:

- Program design lives in spreadsheets that are painful to update across multiple teams and sport seasons
- Athletes receive workouts via PDF, text, or printed sheets — with no way to log progress in context
- Feedback loops are slow: coaches can't see completion rates or athlete questions without chasing people down
- Communication is scattered across email, group chats, and hallway conversations — nothing is centralized
- Scaling across sports is a manual lift: one S&C coach may manage 3–5 teams simultaneously

> The average D1 strength coach spends 30–40% of their time on administrative coordination rather than actual coaching. Freeweight reclaims that time.

---

## The Solution

Freeweight unifies three core workflows:

**Plan** — Build and organize training programs by sport, phase, and athlete. Drag-and-drop simplicity with periodization support.

**Execute** — Athletes see their workouts in a clean, mobile-friendly view. Log sets, reps, and weight in real time. No PDFs, no confusion.

**Communicate** — Inline messaging on workouts. Coaches see completion, flags, and notes at a glance. Teams stay aligned without extra apps.

## Go-To-Market Plan

### Phase 1 — Land
Activate our personal networks within Columbia Athletics. Reach out directly to strength coaches across our respective sports. Lead with our story: we built this because we experienced the problem ourselves. Offer free access in exchange for honest, ongoing feedback.

Target: 3–5 coaches across different sports to maximize the range of use cases we're testing.

### Phase 2 — Learn
Run the product alongside coaches in their actual workflows. Show up to sessions, sit in on planning meetings, be available. The goal isn't just collecting bug reports — it's understanding how coaches think about programming and communication deeply enough to build something they'd never want to give up.

### Phase 3 — Prove
Turn real usage into proof. Document what coaches are doing differently, what athletes are saying, what outcomes have improved. Build case studies with coach permission. These become the asset that opens doors at other schools.

### Phase 4 — Expand
Use Columbia relationships to get warm introductions into other Ivy League programs. Columbia coaches talk to coaches at Penn, Harvard, Yale. A direct referral from a trusted peer is worth more than any sales pitch.

---

## Outreach Playbook

**Initial contact:** Direct, personal outreach from us as athletes — not a sales email. Reference the shared context, name the specific problem, and ask for a conversation. Keep it short.

**First meeting:** Show the product using their sport as the example. Let them interact with it. Focus on the two or three things that would immediately save them time or give them better visibility.

**Pilot kickoff:** Get their existing programs into Freeweight, set up their athlete rosters, and make sure they have a real workout logged before we leave the room. Early momentum is everything.

### Messaging by Audience

| Audience | Core Message |
|---|---|
| Strength Coach | Spend less time on admin. See every athlete's progress without chasing them down. |
| Head Sport Coach | Your athletes are stronger and better prepared. You have visibility into the weight room without being there. |
| Athletic Director | A modern tool that positions Columbia Athletics as a leader in athlete development — at a fraction of enterprise cost. |
| Athletes | Your workouts are clear, your progress is tracked, and your coach can actually see how you're doing. |

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Coaches too busy to engage | Reduce friction to zero — we do the setup, we run the onboarding, we come to them |
| Athletes don't log workouts | In-app reminders; coach dashboard showing completion so coaches can nudge their own athletes |
| Personal relationships don't translate to adoption | Lead with value, not the relationship; the product has to stand on its own |
| Competitive tool enters market | Speed to deep workflow integration; build referral moat before a competitor arrives |
| Coach turnover | Onboard multiple coaches per sport; build relationships at the department level too |
